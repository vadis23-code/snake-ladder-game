Good to Know

Questions Before We Plan Your Celebration?

Do you handle small home events?

Yes. We plan and style intimate home celebrations with the same attention to detail as larger events. No occasion is too small to be beautiful.

Do you manage corporate events?

Absolutely. We handle office inaugurations, product launches, team celebrations, and professional gatherings with polished planning and refined décor.

How early should we book Velora One?

We recommend reaching out 2–4 weeks in advance for most celebrations, and earlier for weddings or large corporate events. For urgent timelines, message us on WhatsApp — we will always try to help.

Can you work within a specific budget?

Yes. Every celebration is custom quoted. Share your budget range with us and we will design the most beautiful experience possible within it.

Do you provide only décor or full event planning?

Both. You can choose décor only, planning plus décor, or complete end-to-end event execution where we manage every detail from concept to wrap-up.

Do you handle apartment clubhouse events?

Yes. Apartment clubhouse celebrations are one of our favourite settings. We coordinate with your community, manage setup timings, and transform the space beautifully.

Do you provide fresh flower decoration?

Yes. We work with fresh florals, artificial arrangements, or a thoughtful blend of both, depending on your style, season, and budget.

Can we share reference photos or themes?

Please do. Reference photos, Pinterest boards, and Instagram saves help us understand your taste, and we build a concept around what you love.

Do you visit the venue before the event?

For most events, yes. A venue visit helps us plan the layout, lighting, and décor placement so everything fits your space perfectly on the day.

How do we start the inquiry process?

Simply message us on WhatsApp at +91 63664 63924, call us, or fill in the inquiry form above. We will respond with ideas and next steps.
